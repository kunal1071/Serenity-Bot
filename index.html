Our assessment uses machine learning algorithms trained on thousands of data points to analyze your responses and provide personalized insights about your mental health state.

Yes, we take your privacy seriously. All your data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent.

No, MindWell is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you're experiencing severe symptoms, please consult with a healthcare professional or therapist.
